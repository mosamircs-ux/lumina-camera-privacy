Privacy Policy

App: Lumina Camera (package com.luminacamera.camera)
Effective date: 19 November 2026
Contact: mosamircs12101996@gmail.com

This policy explains what information Lumina Camera handles and why. It is based on how the app works and the permissions it requests.

1. Summary

  • Lumina Camera does not require an account.
  • Photos and videos you take are saved on your device, in the folder you choose. The developer does not receive them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use advertising identifiers and other data as described in its own policies.
  • Location is used only if you turn on the option to add location to photos and videos.

2. Information the app handles on your device

  • Photos and videos: captured with the camera and saved to your device. The app reads these files to show them and to manage them.
  • Camera and microphone: used to take photos and record video with sound while you use the app.
  • Location (optional): if you turn on "save photo and video location," the app reads your device location and writes it into the metadata of new photos and videos. This setting is off unless you turn it on. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • App settings: your camera preferences (quality, resolution, save location, theme, and similar options) are stored on your device.

3. Information shared with third parties

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device and usage information, including an advertising ID (the Android advertising identifier), IP address, device model, and ad interaction data. This is used to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). Google's handling of data is described in the Google privacy policy.

Photos, videos, and location data stored by the app are not sent to the developer or to advertisers by the app.

5. Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep copies of your media outside your device. To remove app data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Photos and videos you saved remain in the folder you chose until you delete them.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Media and settings stay on your device, protected by your device's security settings. No method of storage is completely secure.
